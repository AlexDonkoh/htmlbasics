BIOGRAPHY OF KOFI KINAATA

Kofi Kinaata is a Ghanaian singer, rapper, and songwriter known for his Fante rap, witty storytelling, and use of proverbs.

Early Life and Education

Born: Martin King Arthur on 15 April 1990 in Effiakuma, a suburb of Takoradi in Ghana’sWestern Region

Education: Attended Nana Brempong Yaw School for primary education, then Takoradi Technical Institute for secondary school

Music Career

Breakthrough: After high school in 2009, he entered Melody FM’s Kasahari rap competition and came 2nd runner-up. The show was known as Ghana’s “unofficial university” for sharp-tongued rappers.

First single: Released “Obi Ne Ba” in 2011, which pushed him from underground to mainstream.

High Grade Family: Joined Samini’s High Grade Family label early on, where he developed his blend of Fante rap, Highlife, and Hiplife. He now runs his own label, Team Move Music

Signature style: Called the Fante Rap God (FRG). He’s known for clean, humorous lyrics packed with Ghanaian proverbs, social commentary, and everyday stories. He writes and sings mostly in Fante, with touches of English.

Notable Songs & Recognition

Hits: “Made in Taadi” 2015, “Confession” 2016, “Susuka”, youtube “Things Fall Apart”,youtube “Sweetpie” youtube, “Adam & Eve”, “Effiakuma Love”

Academic Nod: “Confession” was adopted by KNUST’s English department for a Level 200 literature course. The Asantehene, Otumfuo Osei Tutu II, also quoted “Susuka” at a Ghana Bar Association conference

Awards: Won Songwriter of the Year at the Ghana Music Awards three times consecutively. Also won Hybrid Song of the Year at the 2019 Ghana National Gospel Music Awards.

Beyond Music

Kofi Kinaata is a philanthropist and has started charitable projects in Takoradi. He’s still active, releasing his 2024 EP Kofi OO Kofi with tracks like “Auntie Ama”, “Abonsam”, and “Overthinking”.

Some of the music platforms you can download his musics are: Boomplay, Spotify, Audiomack, Beatport etc.
