Admissions

The Cloud College of Ghana admits students from Ghana and other parts of the world, representing diverse perspectives, experiences, backgrounds and cultures. Ranked as Ghana’s top institution and a global leader in the Times Higher Education Impact Rankings, the University of Ghana offers unparalleled academic excellence. Join a vibrant, multicultural community of over 1,500 international scholars from 71 countries, fostering a truly global perspective. Our commitment to accessibility ensures that qualified students with limited finances receive support through the Students Financial Aid Office. By choosing UG, you access world-class research facilities, pioneering exchange programmes, and a legacy of leadership that empowers you to thrive and innovate in a competitive global landscape.

Undergraduate Admissions

We invite prospective students to apply for undergraduate admission within the current application window. From cutting-edge STEM fields and transformative health sciences to rich humanities, forward-looking education and professional law degrees, the University of Ghana offers an expansive array of programmes designed to cater to every academic passion and career aspiration. Join a world-class community and gain the expertise needed to lead and innovate in a global landscape. Earn a bachelor's degree in your area of interest by choosing from over 100 departments at the University of Ghana.
